Book recommendations and full reading lists from 242 K-pop idols — 1244 books in all, 219 of them read by more than one person. Each entry links to the interview, YouTube video or SNS post it came from.

Looking for a kpop idol reading list? This page collects what kpop idols read, from BTS, SEVENTEEN, IVE and aespa members to solo singers, with the source for every book.

These are Korean stars, so most of the books are Korean editions. Where an official English translation exists it is shown; otherwise the Korean title is kept next to a working translation so you can search for it directly.
